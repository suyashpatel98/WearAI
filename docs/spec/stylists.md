# Stylists

The launch list of Stylists and the brief for each one. Decided in [Which Stylists launch, and what does each one mean?](https://github.com/suyashpatel98/WearAI/issues/2).

## Rules

- A Stylist decides the **aesthetic**: shapes, palette, fabrics and signature pieces. The Feed doesn't dress the User for particular occasions, and WearAI doesn't ask which occasions they dress for. A User who wants an Outfit for a specific occasion (a wedding, an interview) asks for it in Style Me.
- Every Stylist works for both menswear and womenswear. The User's Style Profile (gender, or the "Which clothes should we suggest?" follow-up) decides which notes apply.
- A Stylist is a label: a name, a one-line tagline and sample images in the picker. There is no character, avatar or voice.
- Each Stylist has two sample images, menswear and womenswear. They are generated once on a fictional model and labelled "AI-generated". The picker shows the one that matches the User's clothes setting ("Both" shows both).
- A User has **1 to 3** active Stylists. They pick at least one during onboarding (nothing is pre-selected) and can change them in Settings. Changes apply from the next day's Feed.
- The day's 5 Posts are split evenly across the active Stylists:
  - 1 active: 5 Posts.
  - 2 active: 3 + 2. The Stylist that gets 3 alternates daily.
  - 3 active: 2 + 2 + 1. The Stylist that gets 1 rotates daily.
  - Saves and dismissals don't change the split.
- Season follows the month (see the Style Profile decision). The generator adapts each brief to it, for example lighter fabrics in summer and layers in winter.

## Briefs

### 1. Indian Traditional

_Tagline: Classic Indian dressing, done properly._

Outfits rooted in classic Indian garments, worn the traditional way. Pieces are complete, coordinated sets, with rich fabrics, considered drape and festive colour. Accessories are traditional (juttis, kolhapuris, jhumkas, bangles, a stole or dupatta) and kept in proportion. No western pieces mixed in. That is Indo-Western's job.

- **Signature pieces:** kurta-pyjama, sherwani, bandhgala, Nehru jacket, dhoti; saree, anarkali, lehenga, salwar kameez, sharara
- **Palette:** jewel tones (maroon, emerald, royal blue, mustard), ivory and gold, pastels for daytime
- **Fabrics/textures:** silk, brocade, cotton-silk, chanderi, georgette, zari and thread embroidery
- **Avoid:** western bottoms or footwear, logo or graphic pieces, costume-like over-styling
- **Menswear notes:** kurta-pyjama or churidar for everyday wear; bandhgala or sherwani for weddings; juttis or mojaris
- **Womenswear notes:** a saree or anarkali for festivals; a lehenga for weddings; salwar kameez or a kurta set for office

### 2. Indo-Western

_Tagline: Indian pieces, western ease._

Fusion Outfits that pair one clearly Indian piece with western pieces, or put a western silhouette in Indian fabric. The result should look deliberate and modern, never like two outfits worn at once. Usually one Indian hero piece per Outfit.

- **Signature pieces:** a short kurta with jeans, a Nehru jacket over a shirt or tee, dhoti pants, a bandhgala with trousers; a crop top with an ethnic skirt, a kurta over palazzos or flared pants, a cape-style jacket, a saree with a belt or shirt
- **Palette:** muted earth tones with one accent colour, indigo, off-white
- **Fabrics/textures:** cotton, linen, denim, block print, light embroidery
- **Avoid:** full traditional sets (that's Indian Traditional), more than two Indian pieces in one Outfit
- **Menswear notes:** a kurta with jeans and sneakers or loafers; a Nehru jacket as the bridge piece
- **Womenswear notes:** an ethnic skirt with a crop top or shirt; a kurta with western bottoms; a modern saree drape

### 3. Handloom & Craft

_Tagline: Indian craft, everyday modern._

Outfits built around Indian handloom and craft textiles, worn in simple modern shapes for everyday life. The fabric is the hero: visible weave, natural dyes and hand-printed patterns. Silhouettes stay relaxed and uncluttered so the textile reads clearly.

- **Signature pieces:** a khadi shirt or kurta, an ikat jacket, block-printed shirts and dresses, a handloom saree, a chanderi kurta, a jute or leather sling bag, kolhapuris
- **Palette:** indigo, madder red, natural undyed tones, rust, olive
- **Fabrics/textures:** khadi, ikat, ajrakh, bagru and dabu block print, chanderi, mangalgiri, kalamkari
- **Avoid:** synthetic shine, heavy embellishment, logos, fast-fashion trend pieces
- **Menswear notes:** a khadi or ikat shirt with cotton trousers; a handloom kurta with a jacket for formal wear
- **Womenswear notes:** a handloom saree or a block-printed kurta set; a craft-textile dress or top with simple bottoms

### 4. Smart Casual

_Tagline: Neat, versatile, always appropriate._

Clean, tailored western Outfits that work almost anywhere: never sloppy, never stiff. Well-fitted basics in classic colours, with one small point of interest (a texture, a watch, a good shoe). This is the safe, reliable choice.

- **Signature pieces:** chinos, oxford shirts, polos, a knit sweater, an unstructured blazer, loafers and clean leather sneakers; a shirt dress, tailored trousers with a blouse, a midi skirt, ballet flats or block heels
- **Palette:** navy, white, khaki, grey, olive, light blue
- **Fabrics/textures:** cotton twill, oxford cloth, piqué, light wool, linen blends
- **Avoid:** graphic tees, distressed denim, athletic wear, loud prints
- **Menswear notes:** chinos and an oxford shirt; a blazer added for office
- **Womenswear notes:** tailored trousers and a blouse; a shirt dress; a midi skirt and a knit

### 5. Minimalist

_Tagline: Fewer pieces, cleaner lines._

Pared-back Outfits with clean lines and a neutral palette. Every piece is plain and well cut; interest comes from proportion and fabric, not decoration. Usually two or three pieces plus shoes, with little or no jewellery.

- **Signature pieces:** a plain crew-neck tee, a straight-leg trouser, a crisp white shirt, a long overshirt, minimal sneakers or sandals; a slip dress, a wide-leg trouser, a structured top, a long coat in winter
- **Palette:** black, white, grey, beige, camel; at most one muted colour
- **Fabrics/textures:** cotton, linen, wool, matte finishes
- **Avoid:** prints, logos, embellishment, more than one colour, layered jewellery
- **Menswear notes:** a monochrome tee and trousers; a white shirt with dark trousers
- **Womenswear notes:** a column of one colour; a slip dress with a simple layer

### 6. Quiet Luxury

_Tagline: Looks expensive without trying._

Refined, understated Outfits that signal quality through fabric and fit rather than branding. Soft tailoring, natural fibres and muted tones; relaxed but polished. Nothing shouts, and there are no visible logos.

- **Signature pieces:** a linen shirt, a fine-knit polo or crew neck, pleated trousers, suede loafers, a cashmere-look sweater over the shoulders; a silk blouse, wide-leg trousers, a knit set, a simple gold chain, a structured leather bag
- **Palette:** cream, oatmeal, camel, navy, chocolate, soft grey
- **Fabrics/textures:** linen, fine knits, silk, suede, merino-look wool
- **Avoid:** logos, bright colours, distressing, trendy statement pieces, athletic wear
- **Menswear notes:** a knit polo and pleated trousers with loafers; a linen suit for daytime weddings
- **Womenswear notes:** a silk blouse with wide-leg trousers; a knit set; a simple gold jewellery accent

### 7. Streetwear

_Tagline: Oversized, layered, sneaker-first._

Relaxed, urban Outfits built around oversized shapes, layering and statement sneakers. Graphic pieces are welcome but one per Outfit. The feel is confident and current, not sloppy.

- **Signature pieces:** oversized and graphic tees, hoodies, cargos, wide-leg jeans, a varsity or bomber jacket, an overshirt, chunky sneakers, a cap or bucket hat, a crossbody bag
- **Palette:** black, white, grey, washed colours, one bold accent
- **Fabrics/textures:** heavy cotton jersey, fleece, denim, nylon
- **Avoid:** formal tailoring, traditional pieces, more than one graphic per Outfit
- **Menswear notes:** an oversized tee with cargos and chunky sneakers; a hoodie under a jacket
- **Womenswear notes:** a cropped or oversized tee with cargos or wide jeans; a hoodie dress with sneakers

### 8. K-Style

_Tagline: Soft layers, Seoul-inspired._

Korean-inspired Outfits: soft layering, relaxed wide shapes and gentle colour. Neat and youthful, with attention to proportion (a tucked shirt, a long cardigan, wide trousers). Polished but never stiff.

- **Signature pieces:** wide-leg trousers, an oversized shirt, a cardigan, a sweater vest, a long coat, loafers or clean sneakers; a pleated skirt, a knit vest over a blouse, a long cardigan, Mary Janes
- **Palette:** pastels (sage, sky blue, lilac, butter yellow), cream, grey, beige
- **Fabrics/textures:** soft knits, cotton poplin, light wool, corduroy
- **Avoid:** loud prints, heavy embellishment, tight fits
- **Menswear notes:** wide trousers, a tucked shirt and an open cardigan; a sweater vest over a shirt
- **Womenswear notes:** a pleated skirt with a knit vest; wide trousers with an oversized shirt

### 9. Athleisure

_Tagline: Gym-ready, street-worthy._

Sporty, comfortable Outfits in performance and knit fabrics, styled to work outside the gym too. Clean and coordinated, never just old workout clothes.

- **Signature pieces:** joggers, a track jacket, a zip-up, performance tees, running or trainer sneakers, a cap; leggings or flared leggings, a sports bra worn under a jacket or overshirt, a co-ord tracksuit
- **Palette:** black, grey, white, navy, one bright accent
- **Fabrics/textures:** technical knits, fleece, nylon, stretch jersey
- **Avoid:** formal pieces, traditional pieces, anything that looks worn out
- **Menswear notes:** joggers, a performance tee and a track jacket; running shoes
- **Womenswear notes:** leggings with an oversized zip-up; a co-ord set with trainers. A sports bra is always shown under a covering layer

### 10. Bollywood Glam

_Tagline: Dress to be noticed._

Statement Outfits with shine, bold colour and drama, in the spirit of a film-premiere or sangeet look. One clear hero piece per Outfit (sequins, a dramatic silhouette, a bold colour), with confident accessories. Can be Indian, western or fusion, as long as it's celebratory.

- **Signature pieces:** a velvet or brocade bandhgala, a printed silk shirt, a sequinned jacket, a statement sherwani; a sequinned saree, a cape lehenga, a cocktail gown, statement earrings, embellished heels or juttis
- **Palette:** gold, metallics, deep red, emerald, fuchsia, black
- **Fabrics/textures:** sequins, velvet, satin, silk, metallic brocade
- **Avoid:** plain everyday basics, athletic wear, more than one hero piece
- **Menswear notes:** a velvet bandhgala or printed silk shirt; a sequinned jacket over black
- **Womenswear notes:** a sequinned saree or cape lehenga; a cocktail gown with statement earrings
